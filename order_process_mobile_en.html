How We Process Your Order

Below is the complete workflow, from your first email to the delivery of your completed translation and original documents, if required.

Step 1

Send your request by email

Send us your documents and specify the target language, preferred deadline, and any formatting requirements.

Step 2

Order Review & Confirmation

We review your documents, confirm the total price and turnaround time, and issue an invoice. Once payment is received, we confirm your order and begin work.

Step 3

Translation Process

A professional translator works on your documents, paying close attention to terminology, formatting, and industry standards.

Step 4

Official Certification

Once reviewed and quality-checked, we certify your translation with our official company seal.

Step 5

Digital PDF Copy

We generate a high-resolution PDF scan of your certified translation for easy digital use.

Step 6

Get your translation

You receive the final PDF by email together with your order details and any necessary instructions.

Step 7

Collect your documents

If you need hard copies, you can collect your original documents and certified translation from our office.

Payment / Documents

Deadlines / Urgent Translation

📅 Most translations are completed on the same business day.

⏱ During regular working hours, standard turnaround is 1–2 hours.

⚡ Express translation service is available. The deadline depends on the volume and language.

Quality / Confidentiality

✅ Every translation undergoes a thorough review by our lead translator.

🔒 We guarantee the confidentiality of your documents at every stage.

♻ Unclaimed hard copies are securely destroyed after the storage period.
